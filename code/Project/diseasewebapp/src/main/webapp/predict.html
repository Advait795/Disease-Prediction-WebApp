Process Description

A health risk assessment form collects a comprehensive set of data to evaluate an individual's risk for various health conditions. The form includes:


  • Demographic Details:
    • Sex
    • Age

  • Specific Health Conditions:
    • Hypertension
    • Heart Disease

  • Key Health Metrics:
    • BMI (Body Mass Index)
    • HbA1c Level (indicating average blood glucose over the past three months)
    • Current Blood Glucose Level
    • Average Glucose Level
    • Smoking History

  • Cardiovascular Data:
    • Chest Pain Type (cp)
    • Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps)
    • Cholesterol Level (chol)
    • Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs)
    • Resting Electrocardiographic Results (restecg)
    • Maximum Heart Rate Achieved (thalach)
    • Exercise-Induced Angina (exang)
    • ST Depression Induced by Exercise (oldpeak)
    • Slope of the Peak Exercise ST Segment (slope)
    • Number of Major Vessels Colored by Fluoroscopy (ca)
    • Thalassemia Status (thal)

  • Socioeconomic Factors:
    • Marital Status
    • Work Type
    • Residence Type

The form uses this detailed information to calculate risk scores for conditions like cardiovascular diseases and diabetes, allowing healthcare providers to identify high-risk individuals and develop targeted, personalized interventions.
